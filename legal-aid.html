Free Legal Help

Outstanding warrants, missing ID, unpaid fines, eviction notices — legal issues trap people in homelessness. These organizations provide free legal help to people who need it most.

You do not need money to get legal help. Every organization listed here provides free services to people who qualify. Missing ID and outstanding warrants are among the most common barriers to getting off the street. These organizations exist specifically to help remove those barriers.
