Find a Church Near You

Churches across the Phoenix metro area offer community, hot meals, clothing, shelter assistance, and support — often with no requirements and no judgment.

The best way to find a church close to where you are right now is to contact one of the organizations below. Tell them your location and what you need and they will connect you with the closest option available.

You do not need to be religious to walk through the door. Most churches serving the homeless community welcome everyone regardless of background or belief.
