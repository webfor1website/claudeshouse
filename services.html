Our Services

A Bed

40 single occupancy rooms. Dignity not dormitory. Each room is private, clean, and safe. No shared sleeping spaces. No overcrowding.

Hot Meals

Three meals daily from our community kitchen. Breakfast, lunch, and dinner prepared with care. No one leaves Claude's House hungry.

Laundry

Washers and dryers open 24 hours. Free detergent provided. Clean clothes are dignity. We make sure you have them.

Showers

Clean, private, open 24 hours. Hot water, clean towels, soap. A chance to wash away the day and start fresh.

Fitness

A real gym. Because your body matters. Exercise equipment, weights, space to move. Physical health supports mental health.

Community

People who understand. No judgment. Common spaces for connection, conversation, and support. You're not alone here.

Mental Health Support

Two private rooms available for one on one mental health support sessions. No appointments required. When you need to talk, someone is here to listen.

Job & Skills Support

A job board and weekly skills workshops. When you're ready. Not before. Resume help, interview prep, computer skills. We help you build the future you want.
