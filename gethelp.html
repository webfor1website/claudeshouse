What to Expect When You Arrive

1. Welcome

You'll be greeted by staff who are here to help, not judge. We'll ask your name and what you need right now.

2. Immediate Needs

If you need food, a shower, or laundry, we'll get you set up immediately. No waiting.

3. A Safe Space

If you need a bed, we'll find you one. Single occupancy. Private. Safe.

4. No Pressure

We won't force you into programs or paperwork. You take it at your pace. We're here when you're ready.
