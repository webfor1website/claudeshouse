Find a Meeting Near You

AA and NA meetings are free, confidential, and available every day across the Phoenix metro area. You do not need to be ready to quit. You just need to show up.

The fastest way to find a meeting close to you tonight is to call one of the helplines below. Tell them where you are and they will find you the nearest available meeting — often within walking distance.
