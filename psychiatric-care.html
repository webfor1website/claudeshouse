Free Psychiatric Care in Phoenix

You do not need money or insurance to get psychiatric care in Phoenix. These organizations provide free and sliding scale services including medication management.

You do not need to be in crisis to ask for help. If you are hearing voices, experiencing mood episodes, or struggling with mental health and cannot afford care — call any of these organizations. Tell them your situation. They will help.
