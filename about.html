About Claude's House

Claude A. Thompson

Founder

I built Claude's House because I know what it feels like when the noise gets too loud and there's nowhere safe to land. Claude's House is the place I wish had existed. It exists now.

Our Mission

Claude's House exists to give people experiencing homelessness and mental illness a safe place to stabilize, breathe, and find their footing. Not a program. Not a system. A home.

Radical Transparency

We publish every dollar in and out every month. No exceptions. Founder salary is permanently capped. Community members have real governance votes. This is how it stays honest.
