Serving Phoenix, Scottsdale, Tempe, Mesa, Chandler, Gilbert, Glendale, Peoria and surrounding Maricopa County communities.

Claude's House connects people experiencing homelessness with emergency shelter, free meals, mental health resources, legal aid, AA and NA meetings, cooling centers, job assistance, and community support across the Phoenix metro area. Whether you need shelter tonight, free food, a place to shower, help with a warrant, or just someone to talk to — we are here. Free. Confidential. No judgment.
